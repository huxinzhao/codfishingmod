cod fishing mod
Author: florain
Version: 1.5.4

Summary
Meet new sea creatures and discover fishing stories in Stardew Valley. Includes new fishing quests and a special fish pond. English and Simplified Chinese supported.

Description
Bring your fishing rod and meet some unusual new residents of the ocean.
Watch your mailbox and quest journal for new fishing stories, and give a special catch a home in your fish pond.

Requirements
Stardew Valley 1.6.15+
SMAPI 4.5.1+
No other mods required.

Installation
Extract the cod fishing mod folder into Mods, then launch through SMAPI.
For updates, back up your save and replace the previous installation.
Remove the old [CP] KeniOctopus and KeniOctopus Quest Runtime folders if present.
Legacy special-order progress is no longer migrated automatically; finish those orders on the old version or migrate and save with 1.4.0–1.5.0 before updating.

Validation
Build and automated checks passed. Not tested in-game by the authoring assistant.

Source
https://github.com/huxinzhao/codfishingmod
