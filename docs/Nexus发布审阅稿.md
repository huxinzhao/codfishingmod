## 详情页（英文）

MORE COD-INSPIRED FISH

There are some unfamiliar faces beneath the waves.

Inspired by Call of Duty characters, these unusual sea creatures bring a little wonder to your life in Stardew Valley.

• New ocean catches with their own personalities and pixel art.
• Special fish ponds with distinctive water colors and unexpected produce.
• Aquarium support, so your catches can settle into your farmhouse, too.
• New gift preferences: someone in town might appreciate your latest catch.
• English and Simplified Chinese, following your game's language setting.

REQUIREMENTS

• Stardew Valley 1.6.15 or later
• SMAPI 4.5.1 or later
• Content Patcher 2.9.0 or later — required starting with mod version 1.6.0

INSTALLATION

1. Install SMAPI and Content Patcher.
2. Close the game and back up your save before updating.
3. If updating, remove the previous cod fishing mod folder before extracting the new version. Do not merge the old and new folders. Remove the legacy [CP] KeniOctopus and KeniOctopus Quest Runtime folders if present.
4. Extract the cod fishing mod folder from the download into your game's Mods folder. Keep its Content and Runtime subfolders together.
5. Launch the game through SMAPI.

Current ordinary quest progress and existing fish are preserved when updating. If you still have an unfinished special order from a much older version, finish it on that version first, or migrate and save using version 1.4.0–1.5.0 before updating.

SMAPI can notify you when an update is available on Nexus; download and install the update yourself.
