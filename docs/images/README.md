# Promotional images

Vanilla Stardew Valley art belongs to ConcernedApe. Custom fish sprites belong to Florain. These images are manually composited from the game assets and the mod's sprites; no AI image generation was used.

- `ocean.png`: small crop from the [vanilla Beach overview](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/mediawiki/images/e/e4/Beach_Overview.jpg), with enlarged mod sprites.
- `ponds.png`: vanilla fish pond and item sprites, with water colors and produce taken from this mod's configuration.
- `aquarium.png`: vanilla Aquatic Sanctuary and hats, with all seven mod fish. The hats are promotional decoration, not an added gameplay feature.
